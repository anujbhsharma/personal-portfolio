The problem

Overthinking isn’t a character flaw — it’s a loop. The same worry replays at 2am, the same conversation gets rehearsed eleven times, and “just stop thinking about it” has never once worked. Willpower can’t break a loop because the loop is the thinking. What breaks it is getting the thoughts out of your head and onto paper, where you can actually look at them.

How this helps

Thought Loops applies the core CBT technique — the thought record — in a workbook you work through with a pen, ten minutes a day. You learn to spot the loop, write down the thought, check it against common distortion patterns, and write a calmer, fairer version. A 7-day worry planner gives rumination a scheduled appointment instead of a 2am ambush. A gentle exposure ladder helps you approach avoided situations in small steps. A 21-day tracker with weekly review prompts keeps the practice honest.
